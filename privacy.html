Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

FABLE Creator Studio helps users create, review, and publish short-form content to their own connected social media accounts.

Information We Process

We may process basic account information provided through connected platforms, authentication tokens, publishing selections, captions, and content chosen by the user.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to authenticate the user, display connected account information, prepare user-requested publishing actions, and complete actions explicitly approved by the user.

User Control

FABLE Creator Studio does not publish content automatically. Users must review and approve each publishing action.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information is shared with connected platforms only as necessary to perform actions requested by the user.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect account credentials and authentication tokens.

Data Retention

Information is retained only as long as necessary for authentication, security, legal, and operational purposes.

Third-Party Platforms

TikTok and other connected services process information according to their own privacy policies.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the FABLE Creator Studio operator.
